Gameplay loop

How a trial plays out

  1. Trial docket opened. Read the incident fact pattern and the threat's HP and elements.
  2. Party inspection. Review the 3–5 Republic Acts drafted into your hand. Inspect any of them for the full Gazette record.
  3. Tactical decision. Invoke a Hero Statute, or play a procedural Action Card: Element Check, Request Clarification, Exception Shield, Reshuffle, Search Precedent.
  4. Adjudication. Match: the element is established and the threat takes damage. Misapplication: objection sustained, you lose Juris Power.
  5. Case resolution. Prove every element and bring the threat to 0 HP. The trial is logged and your rank advances.
