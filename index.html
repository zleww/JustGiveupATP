Real statutes, real stats

Every Hero Card is a law from the L-Lawliet database. Its power comes from the law's actual minimum fine and impact rating.

Prove every element

Each case breaks down into the elements a lawyer must establish, each one tied to the section that covers it.

Party synergies

Your record is saved

Sign in with your L-Lawliet account to save every trial, climb the ranks and appear on the leaderboard. Guests can try Tier 1.

Gameplay loop

How a trial plays out

  1. Trial docket opened. Read the incident fact pattern and the threat's HP and elements.
  2. Party inspection. Review the 3–5 Republic Acts drafted into your hand. Inspect any of them for the full Gazette record.
  3. Tactical decision. Invoke a Hero Statute, or play a procedural Action Card: Element Check, Request Clarification, Exception Shield, Reshuffle, Search Precedent.
  4. Adjudication. Match: the element is established and the threat takes damage. Misapplication: objection sustained, you lose Juris Power.
  5. Case resolution. Prove every element and bring the threat to 0 HP. Signed-in players get the trial saved and their rank goes up.
